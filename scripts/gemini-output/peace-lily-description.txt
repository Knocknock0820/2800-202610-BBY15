# Botanical Care Guide: *Spathiphyllum* (Peace Lily)

The **Peace Lily** (*Spathiphyllum*) is a popular indoor houseplant known for its elegant white spathes and lush, dark green foliage. Native to the tropical rainforests of the Americas, this plant is highly valued for its ability to thrive in low-light conditions and its reputation as an effective air purifier.

---

## Overview
*   **Scientific Name:** *Spathiphyllum* spp.
*   **Family:** Araceae
*   **Type:** Tropical perennial
*   **Growth Habit:** Clumping, upright
*   **Native Habitat:** Tropical regions of the Americas and Southeast Asia
*   **Key Feature:** Produces white, hood-like bracts (spathes) surrounding a spadix.

---

## Light
Peace Lilies are prized for their ability to tolerate **low to medium indirect light**.
*   **Optimal Conditions:** Bright, filtered light (e.g., near an east-facing window).
*   **Low Light:** They will survive in darker corners, though blooming may be reduced or inhibited.
*   **Avoid:** Direct sunlight. Harsh afternoon sun can scorch the leaves, causing brown, crispy patches.

## Water
The Peace Lily is famous for its dramatic "fainting" behavior; it will visibly wilt when it requires water.
*   **Frequency:** Keep the soil consistently moist but never waterlogged. Allow the top inch of soil to dry out before watering again.
*   **Water Quality:** These plants are sensitive to chemicals found in tap water, such as fluoride and chlorine. If leaf tips turn brown, consider using **distilled or filtered water**, or allow tap water to sit out overnight before use.
*   **Humidity:** As a tropical plant, it appreciates high humidity. Mist the leaves regularly or place the pot on a tray of pebbles and water to increase ambient moisture.

## Soil
A high-quality, **well-draining potting mix** is essential to prevent root rot.
*   **Recommended Mix:** A standard peat-based potting mix amended with perlite or orchid bark to improve aeration.
*   **pH Requirements:** Prefers slightly acidic soil (pH 5.8–6.5).
*   **Drainage:** Ensure the container has adequate drainage holes; the plant should never sit in standing water.

---

## Common Problems

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yellowing Leaves** | Overwatering or poor drainage | Check soil moisture; ensure pot has drainage holes. |
| **Brown Leaf Tips** | Low humidity or chemical sensitivity | Increase misting; use distilled or filtered water. |
| **No Blooms** | Insufficient light | Move the plant to a brighter location (indirect light). |
| **Drooping Leaves** | Underwatering | Provide a thorough watering; leaves will recover within hours. |
| **Pests (Scale/Aphids)** | Infestation | Wipe leaves with a damp cloth or use neem oil. |

---

## Pet Safety
**Warning:** The Peace Lily is **toxic to cats and dogs**. 

The plant contains **insoluble calcium oxalate crystals**. If ingested, these crystals are released into the mouth and digestive tract, causing immediate irritation, swelling of the lips and tongue, excessive drooling, and difficulty swallowing. 

*   **Recommendation:** Keep this plant in a location inaccessible to curious pets. If ingestion is suspected, contact your veterinarian immediately.
